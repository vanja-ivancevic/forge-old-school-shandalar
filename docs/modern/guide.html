Quick Tips for New Players

Choose Your Color Wisely

Each color starts with a different preconstructed deck and faces different early-game enemies. Green and White are generally more forgiving for beginners.

Explore Every Town

Towns sell cards and offer quests. Visit shops frequently and buy cards that strengthen your deck's strategy rather than just adding powerful cards.

Save Before Bosses

Boss fights are significantly harder than regular encounters. Save your game before entering any dungeon or lair so you can retry with a different strategy.

Manage Your Deck Size

Keep your deck close to 60 cards. A bloated deck dilutes your key cards. Sell extra cards at towns for gold to buy what you actually need.

Items Are Powerful

Equipment items provide permanent bonuses like extra life, starting cards in play, or mana advantages. Prioritize item-granting encounters.

Try Commander Mode

Once you've completed a standard run, try Commander Mode for a wildly different experience. You build around a legendary creature from the old-border era.

Have questions? The community is always happy to help new players.
