Features

Updated February 2026

♦

6,100+ Legal Cards

The complete old-border card pool from Alpha (1993) through Scourge (2003), spanning 46 editions.

✦

Hand-Crafted Decks

Hundreds of unique enemy decks, each designed to feel like a real opponent from Magic's golden age.

☠

Redesigned Boss Fights

99 unique boss encounters with custom decks, unique rewards, and a completely redesigned endgame.

◆

150+ Items & Equipment

New items to discover as you explore dungeons and defeat enemies across the world.

♛

Commander Mode

Play through the adventure with a Commander deck for a completely different challenge. Honestly, this one is so much fun.

⚔

Historical Arena

Arena opponents use real tournament decks from the 1993–2002 World Championships.

♠

Classic Starter Decks

Start your adventure with a real preconstructed deck from across Magic's history, or choose "Pile" for a random assortment of cards.

✧

Hidden Surprises

Secret encounters and rewards for those who explore thoroughly. No spoilers!

⚡

Active Development

Regular updates with new content, balance changes, and bug fixes from a passionate community.

⌨

Cross-Platform

Runs on Windows, Mac, Linux, and Android. 100% free and open source.
