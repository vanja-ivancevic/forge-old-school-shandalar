6,100+ Legal Cards
The complete old-border card pool from Alpha (1993) through Scourge (2003), spanning 46 editions. 		Hand-Crafted Decks
Hundreds of unique enemy decks, each designed to feel like a real opponent from Magic's golden age.
Redesigned Boss Fights
Bosses themed around classic MTG artists, original 1997 Microprose Shandalar decks, and 26+ new endgame bosses. 		150+ Items & Equipment
New items to discover as you explore dungeons and defeat enemies across the world.
Commander Mode
Play through the adventure with a Commander deck for a completely different challenge. Honestly, this one is so much fun. 		Historical Arena
Arena opponents use real tournament decks from the 1993-2002 World Championships.
Classic Starter Decks
Start with a real 7th Edition starter deck (yes, it's bad, just like the old days) or choose "Pile" for random cards. 		Hidden Surprises
Secret encounters and rewards for those who explore thoroughly. No spoilers!
Active Development
Regular updates with new content, balance changes, and bug fixes from a passionate community. 		Cross-Platform
Runs on Windows, Mac, Linux, and Android. Even works on Steam Deck!
