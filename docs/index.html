The Official Home Page

A complete conversion of Forge's Adventure Mode using only cards from
Magic: The Gathering's golden era (1993-2003)


★ Welcome, Planeswalker! ★ Over 6,000 cards from Alpha to Scourge ★ Hundreds of hand-crafted enemy decks ★ 99 boss fights ★ 280+ unique items ★ 100% free and open source ★ Built into the official Forge game ★ No separate download needed ★ Active development ★



Explore the world of Shandalar with over 6,000 cards spanning Alpha through Scourge, fight hand-crafted enemies with thematic lore-appropriate decks, collect over 150 unique items, and battle your way through an entirely redesigned endgame.

This is a love letter from fans who lived through Magic's first decade.

▶ 100% free and open source. Built into the official Forge game. No separate download needed!
